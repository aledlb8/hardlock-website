The game has two player roles: **fighter** and **SAM site**. In the SAM role you don't fly. You run one launch cell on the ground and shoot at the enemy flight with a missile you design yourself on the tactical screen. A new install starts in the SAM role.

This page is the SAM role's **SANDBOX** mode, picked on the briefing's Mode row. The other two modes, RAID CAMPAIGN and ENDLESS RAIDS, put you in charge of a whole airbase under attack: see [Base defence](/world/base-defence/).

## How the launcher works

- **One cell, unlimited rounds.** The next round is ready **2 s** after each launch.<SimChoice /> While the cell is empty the HUD reads RELOADING.
- **No radar.** The radar, chaff, flare and weapon-switch keys only work in the fighter.
- **The site can't be destroyed.** The enemy pilots never attack it. They patrol 12 km from your site and defend themselves against your rounds, but never shoot back.

## Taking a shot

1. **Look** from the site camera, where every match starts: hold the right mouse button and drag to turn, and use the mouse wheel to zoom.
2. **Uncage the seeker** with <Key k="R" />. A seeker ring appears at the centre of the screen.
3. **Put an aircraft in the ring.** The aircraft nearest the centre of the screen becomes the round's target, and stays designated while it remains on screen and **within 10 km** of the launcher.<SimChoice /> Farther aircraft are ignored. The ring gets heavier when it's locked.
4. **Fire** with <Key k="F" />. Launching cages the seeker again for the next round.

If the launch is refused, the HUD says why: RELOADING (wait 2 s), SEEKER CAGED (press <Key k="R" />) or NO TARGET IN THE SEEKER (put an aircraft inside the ring).

<Key k="V" /> cycles the cameras: the site camera, then the **missile camera** following your round, then the free camera to fly around the fight. The site camera stays put through every launch. See [Cameras](/flying/cameras/#site-camera).

To rearm in this role, use the tactical screen (<Key k="G" /> there, or the REARM button).

## Designing the round

The SAM fires the **custom round**, not a catalog missile. Every value is set on the tactical screen's WEAPON page; changes load into the next round, and **APPLY TO THE ROUND IN THE CELL** rebuilds the one already loaded. All values are sim choices for the sandbox round.

| Group | Setting | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launcher | Launch position X / Z | −2000 to 2000 m | 0 m |
| | Launch position Y (height) | 0 to 500 m | 0 m |
| | Launch velocity X / Y / Z (air launch only) | −300 to 300 m/s | 0 / 0 / 50 m/s |
| Airframe | Dry mass | 10 to 1000 kg | 100 kg |
| | Drag coefficient | 0.01 to 1 | 0.1 |
| | Cross-section | 0.01 to 1 m² | 0.1 m² |
| Propulsion | Thrust (the sustainer) | 1000 to 50,000 N | 10,000 N |
| | Propellant | 10 to 1000 kg | 100 kg |
| | Burn rate | 0.1 to 10 kg/s | 0.5 kg/s |
| Guidance | Guidance | off / on | on |
| | Lead aggressiveness (navigation gain) | 1 to 4 | 4 |
| | Seeker field of view (half-angle) | 5 to 180° | 85° |
| | Proximity fuse | 0 to 75 m | 18 m |
| | Flare rejection (1 = immune) | 0 to 1 | 0.65 |
| Terrain | Terrain avoidance | off / on | on |
| | Minimum clearance | 0 to 400 m | 90 m |
| | Look-ahead | 0.5 to 12 s | 6 s |

- **Launch velocity** only matters for an air launch, and only its overall speed counts: the round always leaves pointed at its target, at 26 m/s or more. A round on the ground ignores it.
- **Seeker field of view:** if the target drifts outside it, the round destroys itself.
- **Terrain avoidance:** the round pulls up when it predicts it will come closer to the ground than the minimum clearance within the look-ahead time.
- **Flare rejection:** the higher it is, the brighter a flare has to be to pull the round off its target. At 0.65 a flare needs about twice the aircraft's brightness.

## The cold launch

A round standing on the ground is **cold-launched**: a gas charge throws it clear of the cell, and the motor lights only once it is in the air. All timings are sim choices.

<Diagram name="cold-launch" />

| Time after launch | What happens | HUD state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 s | **Ejection.** The round leaves the cell nearly vertical, leaning towards the target. Motor cold, guidance off. | EJECT |
| 0.85 s | **Ignition.** The booster lights at **4×** the sustainer thrust. | IGNITION |
| from 0.85 s | **Pitch-over.** The motor turns the round towards the target. | IGNITION |
| by 1.3 s | **Guidance takes over**, once the round is pointing within 55° of the target. | INTERCEPT while the seeker holds the target |
| 2.35 s | **Booster burns out.** Thrust drops to the sustainer value. | |

If you raise the launch position more than about 13.6 m above the ground, the round is air-launched instead and lights almost at once.
