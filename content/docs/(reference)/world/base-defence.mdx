Base defence is the ground launcher's second mode. Raids fly down the valley from the north to destroy your airbase, and you defend it with a search radar and three launchers that carry a limited number of rounds. **Lose the command post and the base falls.**

Pick it on the briefing map: in the SAM role the **Mode** row offers SANDBOX (the [practice flight](/world/ground-launcher/)), RAID CAMPAIGN and ENDLESS RAIDS. The built-in scenarios **Base defence** and **Endless raids** set it up too.

| Mode | How it ends |
| --- | --- |
| Raid campaign | Eight raids. Survive the last one with the command post standing and the base is held. |
| Endless raids | Raids keep coming, each a little bigger, until the command post falls. You play for a score. |

## How a raid plays out

1. **Intel.** A card shows the raid's name, the direction it comes from, a rough count of aircraft and what kinds are in it. The raid appears on the scope **12 s** later<SimChoice />, or at once if you press <Key k="F" />.
2. **The raid.** A siren sounds on the base. The raiders fly their tasks, and you shoot at what you can.
3. **The raid ends** once every raider is down or gone home and no weapon is still falling. A raid still going after 15 minutes is called off.
4. **Aftermath.** The world holds still while you spend points, move a launcher and take an upgrade. **NEXT RAID** (<Key k="Enter" />) opens the next intel card.

When the match ends, the [debrief](/world/scenarios/#the-debrief) opens: **BASE HELD** or **BASE LOST**, the reason, and the raids survived, aircraft destroyed, weapons intercepted, rounds fired and rounds per kill, base integrity and points earned. Endless raids also shows the score. RESTART starts again from the first raid.

## The base

Every building on the base has health. As it drops, an asset smokes, then burns and blackens, then is destroyed and left as a burning wreck. A destroyed depot or fuel farm cooks off in more blasts.

| Asset | HUD | When it is destroyed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Command post | CP | The base is lost. It is hardened and takes **four** bomb hits.<SimChoice /> |
| Search radar | RDR | No scope and no long-range shots until it is rebuilt. One radar-seeking missile destroys it. |
| Missile depot | DEP | Every launcher reloads **three times** slower. |
| Fuel farm, hangars | FUEL, HGR | Base integrity and score only. |
| Long-range launcher | LR | No radar-guided shots, and the rounds on its rails are lost. |
| Short-range launcher | SR | No heat-seeker shots, and the rounds on its rails are lost. |
| Experimental cell | EXP | No custom rounds. |

A bomb does full damage to anything within its burst and nothing past twice that distance. **Base integrity** is the share of the base still standing, each asset weighted by its value.

The top left of the HUD shows the raid, the base integrity and your points, then one row per asset: a health bar, the radar's ON or OFF, DOWN for a destroyed asset, and for each launcher its ready rounds and depot stock with a reload tick. <Key k="C" /> frames the base from behind the command post, looking up the valley the raids come down.

## The base radar

The search radar sweeps all the way round every **4.8 s**<SimChoice /> and shows what it sees on the **base scope** at the bottom left: north up, the base in the middle, the long-range launcher's reach as a dashed ring, your assets, tracks with a leader line and their height, your rounds in flight, and every radar-seeking missile coming in (a triangle).

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <Key k="E" /> | Radar on or off. |
| <Key k="T" /> | Target the track nearest the middle of your view, then the next one. |
| <Key k="U" /> | Drop the target. |
| <Key k="Y" /> | Scope range: 10, 20, 40 or 80 km. |

The top right lists CONTACTS while the radar is on, nearest first. A contact reads **UNKNOWN** until you can see it: within **8 km** of the camera with nothing in the way.<SimChoice /> Then it is named: STRIKE, HUNTER, BOMBER, ESCORT, DECOY, CRUISE or the weapon it is.

<Callout kind="radar" title="On is loud, off is blind">
  Switched on, the radar sees raids far out and the long-range launcher can fire. But every raider's warning receiver hears it, and **radar hunters fire at it**. Switched off (SILENT), nothing hears the base, the scope goes dark, and you see only what is in sight; the list reads IN SIGHT. A low cruise missile hugging the ground is hard for the radar to pick out from the ground behind it anyway.
</Callout>

## The launchers

<Key k="1" />, <Key k="2" /> and <Key k="3" /> select a launcher (<Key k="B" /> steps through them), and <Key k="F" /> fires the selected one. The line at the bottom of the HUD shows the selection, its rounds as ready × and depot stock, and either LAUNCH or why it can't fire, with the key to press next.

| Launcher | Round | Ready + depot | Reload | Aims by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Long range (LR) | Radar-guided round | 4 + 6 | 18 s | The radar's target, out to 36 km |
| Short range (SR) | AIM-9X Block II heat seeker | 4 + 12 | 7 s | The sight, out to 10 km |
| Experimental (EXP) | Your [custom round](/world/ground-launcher/#designing-the-round) | 1 + 6 | 5 s | Its own seeker |

All values are sim choices. A launcher reloads one round at a time from the depot until its rails are full; when the depot stock runs out, it doesn't reload.

- **Long range** needs the radar on and a target from <Key k="T" />. The round climbs steeply out of its canister and the radar steers it until its own seeker takes over. If the target's track has gone stale (MEMORY on its box), the line reads TRACK FADED: let the radar see it again. Two aircraft flying close together share the radar's beam and their tracks take turns fading, so press <Key k="T" /> again to step to the one the radar holds.
- **Short range** fires on what its sight holds. Press <Key k="R" /> to open the sight and put a target in the ring; with a radar target and the sight closed, the seeker is slaved to the radar's target instead (SLAVED TO RADAR). The AIM-9X Block II can lock on after it leaves the rail, but its seeker still has to find heat: a jet or a burning missile is an easier target than a gliding bomb.
- **Experimental** fires the round you designed, with the same seeker controls as the [practice launcher](/world/ground-launcher/#taking-a-shot).

## The raiders

The enemy pilots are the same as in the rest of the game (see [Enemy pilots](/world/enemy-pilots/)), each given a task.

| Intel tag | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| STRIKE | Comes in high, drops low for its run and bombs an asset in sticks of two, up to three passes. While on its run it holds course through warnings and breaks only when a missile is a few seconds away. |
| HUNTER | Waits about 34 km out and fires **radar-seeking missiles** at the base radar when it hears it within 32 km. It holds fire while the radar is silent. |
| BOMBER | Flies high and releases **glide bombs** 26 km out, then turns for home. |
| ESCORT | Flies with the bombers or the strike jets and fights only aircraft. |
| DECOY | A small drone that looks like a fighter on radar and circles out in front of the base. It carries nothing. |
| CRUISE | A **cruise missile** that hugs the valleys, pops up about 3 km from its target and dives onto it. |

Glide bombs, radar-seeking missiles, cruise missiles and decoys can all be shot down; a plain bomb can't.

### Radar-seeking missiles

When a radar-seeking missile is in the air, the HUD shows **RADAR-SEEKING MISSILE INBOUND** with its time to impact and whether the radar is TRANSMITTING or SILENT, and a warble sounds in the headset. The missile homes on the radar while it transmits. **Switch the radar off** and it flies to the last place it heard it, losing that place a little more each second, so a late switch still lets it land close. The **Decoy emitter** upgrade puts a second transmitter 1.5 km in front of the radar; a missile goes for whichever it hears nearer.

## Between raids

The aftermath screen shows how the raid went, your points and the base plan.

- **Points.** You start with 300 and earn 200 for each raid survived, plus each kill: 100 for a strike jet or escort, 150 for a hunter, 200 for a bomber, 50 for a cruise missile, 40 for a radar-seeking missile, 25 for a decoy, 20 for a glide bomb.
- **REPAIR** mends an asset to full; a destroyed one is rebuilt. It costs the asset's value times the share that was lost: command post 600, radar and depot 350, hangars and LR 250, fuel farm 200, SR 150, EXP 100.
- **REARM** buys rounds into the depot: 2 long range for 120 points, 4 short range for 80, 3 experimental for 30.
- **Move a launcher.** Click LR or SR on the base plan, then click where it should go: on the base, on dry ground, and clear of everything else.
- **Pick one upgrade** of three offered.

All prices and values are sim choices.

| Upgrade | Effect | Times |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faster scan | The radar sweeps 20 % faster. | 2 |
| Radar power | The radar sees a quarter further. | 2 |
| Extra ready round | One more round on the LR and SR rails. | 2 |
| Faster reload | Launchers reload a quarter faster. | 2 |
| Decoy emitter | A second transmitter for radar-seeking missiles to go for. | 1 |
| Hardened shelters | Every asset takes about a quarter less from each blast. | 2 |
| Deep magazine | 2 LR, 4 SR and 2 EXP rounds into the depot. | 9 |
| Repair crews | Damaged assets mend during a raid once nothing has hit them for 15 s. | 1 |

## The score

Endless raids ends with a score: **1000** for each raid survived, the points for every kill, **500 × the base integrity** after each raid survived, and up to **300** a raid for shooting well (300 at one round per kill, 100 less for each extra round per kill).

## The campaign

| Raid | What comes |
| --- | --- |
| 1. First light | Two strike jets for the depot. |
| 2. Two axes | Two pairs of strike jets a minute apart, the second further west. |
| 3. Wild weasel | A radar hunter, then two strike jets for the command post. |
| 4. Low and fast | Cruise missiles, a radar hunter behind them, more missiles later. |
| 5. Heavy metal | Two bombers with escorts, releasing glide bombs far out. |
| 6. Ghosts | Decoys, radar hunters among them, strike jets behind. |
| 7. Combined arms | Bombers and escorts, strike jets and cruise missiles on three axes. |
| 8. Last stand | Everything at once, from every side. |
