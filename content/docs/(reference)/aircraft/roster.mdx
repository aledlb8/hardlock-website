The game has thirteen aircraft and **you can fly every one of them**. The F-16C Block 50 is the default and the only jet with a full flight model built on NASA wind-tunnel data. The other twelve are point-mass airframes fed only by the numbers on their catalog card. [How the jet flies](/flying/flight-model/) explains both models.

Enemy aircraft fly the same flight models you do. The enemy flight is an Su-27S leading, then a MiG-29 9.13, an Su-35S and another Su-27S, repeating if there are more aircraft.<SimChoice /> You cannot choose the enemy's aircraft.

## How a card becomes a flying jet

Every card can carry two weights. The **brochure mass** is whatever the public source printed, often a maximum takeoff or an empty weight; it is shown on the card but not flown. The **flown mass** is built the same way for every jet: empty weight, plus half the internal fuel, plus two short-range missiles and a pilot. No aircraft burns fuel, so that weight never changes.

For the point-mass jets:

- **Thrust** is the card's military rating, and the afterburner switches to the maximum rating.
- **Sea-level speed**, where the card states one, is the speed the jet reaches at sea level on full thrust. It is a drag target, not a hard limit.
- **No cell is empty.** Where nothing is published, the card carries an open-source figure or a labelled estimate, and the jet flies it.<SimChoice />
- **No point-mass jet can pass 1,300 m/s.**<SimChoice />

Every jet also has the same heat signature; only its radar cross section differs.

## Loadout

Every jet carries the same stores: two heat seekers, four radar missiles, 120 chaff and 120 flares.<SimChoice /> Any heat seeker and any radar missile fits any jet. Where the missiles ride follows each type's usual air-to-air carriage:<SimChoice />

| Aircraft | Heat seekers | Radar missiles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F-16C, F/A-18E, JAS 39E Gripen, Rafale C, Su-27S | Wingtip rails | Wing pylons, two each side |
| MiG-29 9.13, Su-35S | Outer wing pylons | Wing pylons, two each side |
| Eurofighter Typhoon | Outer wing pylons | Under the fuselage |
| F-15C | Wing pylons | Under the fuselage |
| F-35A | Outer wing pylons | Internal bays |
| F-22A, J-20A, Su-57 | Internal bays | Internal bays |

You see every missile still carried, on your jet and on the enemy's; one in a bay stays hidden. Each one fired is gone from its station until it is rearmed. [Taking the shot](/weapons/launching/#at-launch) describes how each one leaves.

## The hangar card

Point at an aircraft in the hangar or on the tactical screen to open its card. It shows the model type and the card status ("Full table", "Brochure" or "Open-source estimate"), then mass, flown mass, thrust, wing area, load limits, sea-level speed and the radar, with the card's note underneath. **A figure with no official source is drawn faint** and says it is an estimate.

Under the aircraft in the hangar, four stat bars compare it with the fleet: THRUST / WEIGHT, WING LOADING, LOAD LIMIT and SEA-LEVEL SPEED.

In the sections below, "public" means a named public source, "open sources" means press and reference figures with no official card behind them, "published game data" means a figure taken from another game's stat card where public sources leave the cell empty, and "estimate" is a labelled sim choice.

## F-16C Block 50

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/f-16c-block-50.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's F-16C Block 50 model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's F-16C Block 50 model." width={960} height={720} />

The default jet and the only full airframe. Its forces, moments, inertia, control surfaces and engine come from NASA TP-1538, a 1979 wind-tunnel and simulator study of the F-16, with the engine scaled to the Block 50's F110-GE-129.

| Figure | Value | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flown mass | 10.4 t (22,972 lb): 18,900 lb empty + half of 7,000 lb fuel + two AIM-9X + pilot | the same build-up as every jet |
| Military thrust | 14,807.7 lbf at sea level, standing still | NASA engine data, scaled |
| Afterburner thrust | 24,655.2 lbf at sea level, standing still | NASA engine data, scaled |
| Wing area | 27.87 m² (300 ft²) | public, NASA TP-1538 |
| g limits | +9 / −3 | +9 is the published F-16C limit; −3 has no published source |
| Angle of attack, roll rate | 25°, 308 °/s | public, NASA TP-1538 |
| Speed | 1,555 km/h indicated-airspeed limit | published game data; shown on the card, not enforced in flight |
