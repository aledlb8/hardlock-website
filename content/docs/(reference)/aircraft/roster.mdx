The game has thirteen aircraft and **you can fly every one of them**. Each is a full airframe with its own wing, weight, engines and fly-by-wire limits; [How the jet flies](/flying/flight-model/) explains the model. The F-16C Block 50 is the default.

Enemy aircraft fly the same flight models you do. The enemy flight is an Su-27S leading, then a MiG-29 9.13, an Su-35S and another Su-27S, repeating if there are more aircraft. You cannot choose the enemy's aircraft.

## What the numbers mean

- **Mass** is the jet's combat weight, roughly its empty weight plus half its internal fuel, two short-range missiles and a pilot. No aircraft burns fuel, so it never changes.
- **Thrust** falls with height and changes with Mach; the figures are at a standstill at sea level. See [Throttle, afterburner and fuel](/flying/engine-and-fuel/).
- **Sea-level speed and top speed** are where the jet's drag stops it on full afterburner, not hard limits.
- Each jet's **heat** follows its engines: big twins run hotter, and the F-22A's and F-35A's nozzles hide part of the tailpipe. [Heat seekers](/weapons/heat-seekers/#how-hot-a-jet-runs) lists every jet.

## Loadout

Every jet carries the same stores: two heat seekers, four radar missiles, 120 chaff and 120 flares. Any heat seeker and any radar missile fits any jet. Where the missiles ride follows each type's usual air-to-air carriage:

| Aircraft | Heat seekers | Radar missiles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F-16C, F/A-18E, JAS 39E Gripen, Rafale C, Su-27S | Wingtip rails | Wing pylons, two each side |
| MiG-29 9.13, Su-35S | Outer wing pylons | Wing pylons, two each side |
| Eurofighter Typhoon | Outer wing pylons | Under the fuselage |
| F-15C | Wing pylons | Under the fuselage |
| F-35A | Outer wing pylons | Internal bays |
| F-22A, J-20A, Su-57 | Internal bays | Internal bays |

You see every missile still carried, on your jet and on the enemy's; one in a bay stays hidden. Each one fired is gone from its station until it is rearmed. [Taking the shot](/weapons/launching/#at-launch) describes how each one leaves.

## The hangar card

Point at an aircraft in the hangar or on the tactical screen to open its card. Under its name it says whether it is a single-engine or twin-engine fighter, then lists, in this order: mass, engines, dry thrust and afterburner thrust (each, on a twin), wing area, wingspan, load limits, AoA limit, roll rate, thrust vectoring (on the three jets that have it), sea-level speed, top speed, the radar set and its antenna, and the radar's coverage across and up and down. **Every figure is the one the game flies.**

Under the aircraft in the hangar, four stat bars compare it with the fleet: THRUST / WEIGHT, WING LOADING, LOAD LIMIT and SEA-LEVEL SPEED.

## F-16C Block 50

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/f-16c-block-50.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's F-16C Block 50 model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's F-16C Block 50 model." width={960} height={720} />

The default jet, flying NASA wind-tunnel data. It reaches about 1,710 km/h near sea level, well past the 1,555 km/h airspeed limit printed for it, which the game does not enforce, and Mach 1.95 at 11 km. Its 308 °/s roll is the fastest in the fleet.

## F-15C

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/f-15c.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's F-15C model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's F-15C model." width={960} height={720} />

The fastest jet high up, at Mach 2.5. It carries its radar missiles on short launchers under the fuselage.

## F/A-18E

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/fa-18e.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's F/A-18E model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's F/A-18E model." width={960} height={720} />

**The only jet held to +7.5 g.** Its 45° AoA limit lets it point its nose hard when slow, and its 160 °/s roll is the slowest in the fleet.

## F-22A

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/f-22a.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's F-22A model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's F-22A model." width={960} height={720} />

Supercruises at Mach 1.82 on dry power. Pitch thrust vectoring (±20°) and a 60° AoA limit, the highest in the fleet. It carries everything in internal bays and has the smallest radar cross section.

## F-35A

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/f-35a.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's F-35A model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's F-35A model." width={960} height={720} />

One engine and a 50° AoA limit. Its radar missiles ride in internal bays, its heat seekers on outer wing pylons.

## JAS 39E Gripen

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/gripen-e.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's JAS 39E Gripen model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's JAS 39E Gripen model." width={960} height={720} />

The lightest jet, with the lowest thrust-to-weight in the fleet. Its Raven ES-05 radar sits on a repositioner, so it searches ±100° either side of the nose.

## Eurofighter Typhoon

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/typhoon.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's Eurofighter Typhoon model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's Eurofighter Typhoon model." width={960} height={720} />

Supercruises at Mach 1.49 on dry power and rolls at 250 °/s. Its CAPTOR-M searches ±70°, the widest of the mechanical radars.

## Rafale C

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/rafale-c.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's Rafale C model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's Rafale C model." width={960} height={720} />

Its engines spool the fastest in the fleet, and it rolls at 250 °/s. Its RBE2-AA keeps 40 numbered tracks.

## MiG-29 9.13

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/mig-29-9-13.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's MiG-29 9.13 model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's MiG-29 9.13 model." width={960} height={720} />

Second aircraft in the enemy flight. Its N019 radar looks 60° up but only 38° down, and can guide missiles onto **only 2 targets at once**.

## Su-27S

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/su-27s.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's Su-27S model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's Su-27S model." width={960} height={720} />

Lead and fourth aircraft in the enemy flight. Its N001 radar looks 60° up but only 40° down, and can guide missiles onto **only 1 target at a time**.

## Su-35S

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/su-35s.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's Su-35S model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's Su-35S model." width={960} height={720} />

Third aircraft in the enemy flight. Thrust vectoring in pitch and yaw (±15°) and a 44° AoA limit. Its N035 Irbis-E is the only **passive electronically scanned (PESA)** radar, on a repositioner that lets it search ±120° either side of the nose.

## Su-57

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/su-57.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's Su-57 model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's Su-57 model." width={960} height={720} />

Thrust vectoring in pitch and yaw (±16°) and a 50° AoA limit; at low speed it turns as hard as the F-22A. It carries everything in internal bays.

## J-20A

<Figure src="/img/aircraft/j-20a.webp" alt="Grey render of the game's J-20A model in three-quarter front view" caption="The game's J-20A model." width={960} height={720} />

A canard delta that carries everything in internal bays. Its radar has no published name, so the card calls it simply AESA.
