This walkthrough uses the defaults: the **F-16C Block 50**, **AIM-9X Block II** heat seekers on the wingtips, and the game's **reference radar missile**. Each step links to the page that explains it in depth.

## 1. Choose the fighter

A new install starts you in the **SAM site** role, running the ground launcher. To fly instead:

1. On the title screen choose **START ENGAGEMENT**.
2. On the briefing map, under ENGAGEMENT in the column on the right, set **You fly** to **FIGHTER**.
3. Leave the enemy at **2 aircraft** and **VETERAN** skill for now, and press **LAUNCH**.

Picking any jet or missile in the hangar also makes you the fighter. See [Menus and game flow](/world/game-flow/).

## 2. Get your bearings

You start over your own base, flying level towards the first enemy aircraft at 250 m/s and at its altitude (never below 600 m), with the throttle at 85 %.

- **Move the mouse** to place the aim circle. The jet banks and pulls to fly to it on its own.
- Hold <Key k="Shift" /> or <Key k="Ctrl" /> to move the throttle, and press <Key k="X" /> for afterburner.
- Hold the right mouse button to look around without turning the jet.

[Mouse aim and the stick](/flying/mouse-aim/) explains how the circle turns into stick and rudder.

<Callout kind="threat" title="Nothing will pull you up">
  There is no automatic ground avoidance. If you put the circle on a mountain, the jet flies into it. Coming within 1.5 m of the ground is a crash, and water counts as ground.
</Callout>

## 3. Find them on the radar

The radar scope sits at the bottom left. In search it sweeps ±60° in front of you, and contacts appear as small squares as the beam finds them. Press <Key k="Y" /> to step the scope through 10, 20, 40 and 80 km.

A contact appears only once the radar has seen it twice. [How the radar sees](/radar/how-the-radar-sees/) explains why far or small targets come and go.

## 4. Lock one up

1. Put the aim circle near a contact and press <Key k="T" />. That's a **soft lock**: the contact gets a track number and becomes your target, and the radar keeps scanning.
2. Press <Key k="L" /> for a **hard lock**. The beam now stays on that one target. Press <Key k="L" /> again to drop back to the soft lock.
3. <Key k="U" /> steps back one level at a time.

<Callout kind="radar" title="Soft locks are quiet, hard locks are not">
  Enemy pilots ignore your search and your soft lock. A hard lock "spikes" them, and a Rookie or Veteran with no shot of its own in the air will usually turn to beam you. [Track numbers and locks](/radar/locking/) has the full picture.
</Callout>

## 5. Take the shot

<Key k="B" /> switches between the **heat seekers** and the **radar missile**. The weapon line at the bottom of the HUD tells you if you're cleared to fire. When it says **SHOOT**, press <Key k="F" />.

- **Radar missile.** It needs a soft or hard lock whose track is fresh enough (the weapon line reads LOCKING until it is). After launch your radar guides it until its own seeker takes over close in, so keep the target locked. See [Radar missiles](/weapons/radar-missiles/).
- **Heat seeker.** Your radar lock points the seeker at the target. Without a lock, press <Key k="R" /> to uncage the seeker so it searches along the nose. The AIM-9X Block II locks on after launch, so a designated target is enough to fire. See [Heat seekers](/weapons/heat-seekers/).

If a launch is refused, the HUD says why, such as NO LAUNCH-QUALITY TRACK. A kill shows **SPLASH**, and every missile that ends its flight shows **SHOT ENDED** with the reason. See [Fuzes, hits and misses](/weapons/fuze-and-hits/).

## 6. Survive the reply

Before a radar shot, an enemy pilot holds a hard lock on you for 1.5 s, which your warning receiver hears first.

- Hold <Key k="Space" /> for flares against heat seekers.
- Hold <Key k="Z" /> for chaff against radar missiles.
- Turn so the missile is on your wing line with the ground behind you. See [Notching](/survival/the-notch/).

[What makes a shot miss](/survival/what-makes-a-miss/) collects every defence. This first fight is the open sandbox: if you go down, your jet explodes and stays down until you restart from the pause menu, and <Key k="G" /> rearms everything as often as you like. See [Kills, crashes and rearming](/world/outcomes/). For a fight that can be won or lost, pick one from **SCENARIOS** on the title screen; [Scenarios and the editor](/world/scenarios/) lists them.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Press <Key k="Tab" /> whenever you need a breather. The tactical screen pauses the game and shows every enemy aircraft, what it is doing, and what it has left.
</Callout>
