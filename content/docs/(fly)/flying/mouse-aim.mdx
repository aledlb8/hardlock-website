You never fly the stick directly. **The mouse moves an aim circle, and an instructor flies the jet's nose onto it.** On the HUD the circle is where you are asking the nose to go, and the small cross is where the nose points now.

Mouse aim is on whenever you are in the fighter role, in the fighter camera, with no menu open.

## Moving the aim circle

Moving the mouse right always moves the circle right on screen, however the view is rolled.

- **Aim sensitivity** (Settings > Controls) scales how far the circle moves, from 25 % to 300 %.
- **Invert vertical aim** flips up and down.
- While you fly, the cursor is captured and the game reads raw, unaccelerated mouse motion where the system supports it.

## How the jet flies to the circle

The instructor banks so the jet's lift points where the turn needs it, then pulls the nose onto the circle:

- **Circle ahead:** wings level, small smooth corrections.
- **Circle to the side:** a coordinated banked turn.
- **Big correction:** the jet rolls first, then pulls as the bank comes round. It does not reverse halfway through a big roll.
- **Circle well below the nose:** up to about 25° down the jet pushes. Beyond about 35° it rolls over and pulls instead of pushing negative g.
- **Fine aiming:** within a few degrees of the circle it adds a touch of rudder to slide the nose across.

The fly-by-wire (F-16) or the lift cap (other jets) still decides how hard the jet can actually pull.

<Callout kind="threat" title="It will fly you into the ground">
  The instructor has no terrain check and no automatic pull-up. If you put the circle on the ground, the jet flies there.
</Callout>

## What W, S, A, D, Q and E do

The flight keys take one axis away from the instructor for as long as you hold them. The jet's limits still apply.

| Key | Asks for |
| --- | --- |
| <Key k="S" hold /> | A maximum-performance pull |
| <Key k="W" hold /> | A push to −3 g |
| <Key k="A" hold /> / <Key k="D" hold /> | The jet's full roll rate, left or right |
| <Key k="Q" hold /> / <Key k="E" hold /> | 6° of sideslip, left or right |

**Holding a pitch key also takes the roll axis**, so the instructor cannot bank against your manual pull. The pitch keys work like a stick: <Key k="W" /> pushes the nose down.

When you let go, that axis blends back to the instructor over 0.15 s.<SimChoice /> **The circle stays where the mouse left it, so the jet flies back to it.** A bank you set with a quick tap of the roll keys does not stay.

## Free look

Hold <Key k="RMB" hold /> or <Key k="C" hold /> to look around without turning the jet. **The aim circle freezes and dims, and the jet keeps flying to it** while the mouse moves your view. When you let go, the view swings back to the circle.

In free look the flight keys behave differently: while you hold one, the circle rides on the nose, and when you let go the jet holds its new heading instead of turning back.

<Callout kind="tip">
  - Small mouse moves give smooth, wings-level corrections.
  - A big flick makes the jet roll first, then pull, so lead your turns.
  - Hold <Key k="S" /> when you want every bit of pull the jet has.
  - To check your six without losing your heading, use free look.
</Callout>

How quickly the view follows the circle is the Camera lag setting. See [Cameras](/flying/cameras/).
