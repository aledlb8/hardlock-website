The game has **38 heat seekers** and **27 named radar missiles**, plus a fictional **reference** radar round. This page lists the numbers that change how a round plays; the round's card in the hangar shows the same values. A jet can only be armed with its own nation's rounds and the ones its real type is cleared for ([Loadout](/aircraft/roster/#loadout)), so some rounds here are carried by no jet in the game.

## What every heat seeker shares

- **The same fin coefficient**, from the AIM-9B, so a brochure g figure never makes a round turn harder at the same speed and height.
- **The same guidance** (with the hard turn past 40° off the collision course).
- Most rounds are held to **30 g**; the table below lists every round's limit and motor.

[How a missile flies](/weapons/missile-flight/) and [How a missile steers](/weapons/guidance/) explain both.

**Narrow, slow heads.** The AIM-9B (4° field of view, 11°/s), AIM-9D (2.5°, 12°/s), AIM-9H (2.5°, 20°/s) and R-3S (3.5°) hold their lock only inside that narrow field and can lose a hard-turning target. Every other round holds its lock anywhere inside its gimbal.

**Lock range**, against a fighter from dead astern. It shrinks as the aspect moves forward; nose-on, an all-aspect head locks at under half of it ([Heat seekers](/weapons/heat-seekers/#heat-and-aspect)).

- RVV-MD: about 22.4 km. PL-5EII: 16 km.
- Imaging rounds, the R-73 and the R-73M: 18 km.
- Every other all-aspect round: 10 km.
- Rear-aspect rounds, the Magic I and the AIM-9D: 6 km. The AIM-9D sees twice as far when the target is in afterburner.

### Heat-seeker timers

Every heat seeker stops guiding, then destroys itself, after a set time:

| Round | Guidance stops | Self-destructs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AIM-9B | 20 s | 24 s |
| AIM-9D | 60 s | 60 s |
| AIM-9J | 40 s | 40 s |
| R-3S | 21 s | 22 s |
| R-13M | 55 s | 55 s |
| R-60, R-60M | 24 s | 25 s |
| Magic I | 26 s | 26 s |
| R-27T, R-27ET, MICA IR | 60 s | 60 s |
| Every other round | 40 s | 40 s |

A round also flies straight, unguided, for its first 0.4 s (0.2 s with thrust vectoring), except the AIM-9B (0.5 s) and the R-3S (0.6 s).
