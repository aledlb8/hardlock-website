The game has **38 heat seekers** and **27 named radar missiles**, plus a fictional **reference** radar round. In the tables below, **† marks a published figure**. Everything else is a sim choice, an inference or a fictional game profile.<SimChoice /> The in-game card shows each round's sources in full; this page only lists what changes how a round plays.

## What every heat seeker shares

- **The same fin coefficient**, from the AIM-9B, so a brochure g figure never makes a round turn harder at the same speed and height.
- **The same guidance** (with the hard turn past 40° off the collision course).
- Where a g limit was never published, it is **30 g**. Where a motor was never published, the round gets a fictional motor class scaled by its mass.

[How a missile flies](/weapons/missile-flight/) and [How a missile steers](/weapons/guidance/) explain both.

**Narrow, slow heads.** The AIM-9B (4° field of view, 11°/s†), AIM-9D (2.5°, 12°/s†), AIM-9H (2.5°, 20°/s†) and R-3S (3.5°) hold their lock only inside that narrow field and can lose a hard-turning target. Every other round holds its lock anywhere inside its gimbal.

**Lock range**, against a fighter from dead astern. It shrinks as the aspect moves forward; nose-on, an all-aspect head locks at under half of it ([Heat seekers](/weapons/heat-seekers/#heat-and-aspect)).

- RVV-MD: about 22.4 km. PL-5EII: 16 km.
- Imaging rounds, the R-73 and the R-73M: 18 km.<SimChoice />
- Every other all-aspect round: 10 km.<SimChoice />
- Rear-aspect rounds, the Magic I and the AIM-9D: 6 km.<SimChoice /> The AIM-9D sees twice as far when the target is in afterburner.

<Callout kind="sim" title="Radar cards that fly alike">
  - **The eight AIM-120 variants fly as three weights**: A/B/C, C-5/C-7/C-8 and D/D-3. Within a group the cards differ only in what they describe.

**The enemy always fires the reference round**, whatever card you've selected, and the R-73 as its heat seeker.
