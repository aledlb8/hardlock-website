**Every missile in the game carries a proximity fuze.** Heat seekers burst within 3.5 to 12 m, radar missiles within 18 m. Whether a missile kills is then a fixed rule: there is **no kill-probability roll** anywhere.

## Proximity fuzes

| Missile | Fuze radius |
| --- | --- |
| AIM-9B | **9.144 m** (30 ft, published) |
| R-3S | **9 m** (published) |
| R-73 | **3.5 m** (published) |
| R-27T, R-27ET | **12 m**<SimChoice /> |
| Other all-aspect heat seekers | **8 m**<SimChoice /> (R-60M: 5 m) |
| Other rear-aspect heat seekers | **6 m**<SimChoice /> (AIM-9D: 5.2 m, R-60: 5 m) |
| Every radar missile | **18 m**<SimChoice /> |
| Ground launcher's custom round | your setting, 18 m by default |

## What counts as a hit

The game sweeps the missile's path against every aircraft's path, yours included, and takes the first of:

- **Proximity:** an armed missile comes within the aircraft's radius plus its fuze radius.
- **Contact:** the missile touches the aircraft's radius.

Enemy aircraft get a radius between **3 and 7 m**, drawn at random when they spawn. Yours is **5 m**.<SimChoice />

**A proximity fuze can't tell who is who.** It fires on the first aircraft to enter its reach, which isn't necessarily the one the seeker was chasing. A missile ignores the aircraft that fired it until its fuze arms.

## When a fuze arms

A heat seeker arms after a minimum distance flown from the launch point, and on some rounds a minimum time as well:

| Missiles | Arms after |
| --- | --- |
| AIM-9B | 150 m (published) |
| AIM-9D | 304.8 m (1000 ft, published) |
| Other Sidewinders | 304.8 m (the AIM-9D figure, reused) |
| R-3S | 0.4 s after its motor burns out |
| Magic I | 300 m and 1.8 s |
| MICA IR | 500 m (published) |
| Others | 150 m to 600 m (see [the catalog](/weapons/catalog/)) |

A **radar missile** arms once it has flown 300 m or for 1 s, whichever comes first.<SimChoice /> The ground launcher's round is armed from launch.

## How damage works

Every aircraft starts with full structure.

| What happened | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| **Armed missile touches an aircraft** | Destroyed. |
| **Warhead bursts** (proximity, or an armed round hitting the ground or destroying itself) | Full damage inside the fuze radius, measured to each aircraft's surface, falling evenly to zero at **twice** that radius. |
| **Dud**: an unarmed missile touches an aircraft | 60 % of its structure, and no blast. Two duds bring an aircraft down.<SimChoice /> |

<Callout kind="threat" title="Bursts hit everyone in range">
  A burst damages every aircraft inside its reach, including the one that fired it. With an 18 m radar fuze, damage reaches 36 m from the burst to an aircraft's surface.
</Callout>

## SHOT ENDED reasons

Every shot ends exactly once, with one reason. When any shot in the world ends, yours or the enemy's, the HUD shows SHOT ENDED with the reason for **4 s**:

| On screen | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| PROXIMITY FUZE | The proximity fuze fired the warhead. |
| DIRECT HIT | An armed missile struck an aircraft. |
| DUD STRIKE | An unarmed missile struck an aircraft: damage, no blast. |
| HIT THE GROUND | It flew into the terrain. An armed round bursts there. |
| SELF-DESTRUCT | A heat seeker's self-destruct timer ran out, the custom round lost its target, or a MADDOG found nothing in 30 s. |
| OVERSHOT | The ground launcher's custom round flew past its target and the range was opening. |
| OUT OF ENERGY | Motor out, more than 2 s of flight, slower than 15 m/s. |
| LEFT THE ARENA | More than 150 km from the centre or above 30 km. |
| BATTERY SPENT | A radar missile flew for its whole 120 s battery. |
| REMOVED | Cleared by a restart or reset. |

A heat seeker's self-destruct bursts if it is armed. A radar missile ending by MADDOG time-out or by its battery doesn't burst.

<Callout kind="tip" title="In the cockpit">
  - **The R-73 is the least forgiving heat seeker**: it has to pass within 3.5 m of your target's 3 to 7 m radius. Most others forgive 6 to 8 m, and a radar missile forgives 18.
  - Inside the arming distance, a hit is a DUD STRIKE. Two of those still destroy an aircraft.
</Callout>
